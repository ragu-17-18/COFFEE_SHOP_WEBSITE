Small batch,
on purpose

We started roasting in a garage in 2016 because the coffee we loved kept going stale before it reached us.

Now we roast twice a week, in batches small enough that every bag is under ten days old when it's poured. We buy direct from four farms we've visited ourselves, and we pay well above commodity price — not because it's a slogan, but because it's the only way the coffee stays this good.

The shop on Mill Lane opened in 2019, one room with six tables and a roaster in the back you can watch from the counter. Everything you drink here was still green a few days earlier.

How we got here

2016
First batches roasted on a 1kg drum in a garage, sold at the Saturday market.
2018
Started buying direct from growers in Ethiopia and Colombia instead of through brokers.
2019
Opened the shop on Mill Lane, with the roaster moved in behind the counter.
2026
Still small, still roasting twice a week, still pouring every cup to order.
